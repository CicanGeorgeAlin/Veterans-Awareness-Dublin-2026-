The Grandson

Cican
George Alin

I am the grandson of Nicolae Urluescu.

I live in Dublin, Ireland, where I created Veteran Awareness Dublin 2026 as my personal way of remembering my grandfather.

My life is connected with the outdoors. I camp, explore and spend time in nature. I am also a Navy diver.

Through this project, remembrance becomes something we can carry forward through creativity, awareness and peace.

From Memory To Art

Remember
Through Color

The project transforms remembrance, personal memories and veteran awareness into something people can experience through creativity.

The intention is simple: remember the past, respect those who served, and create something peaceful for the future.

€1

From every coloring book, approximately €1 of profit is intended to go to the Irish Veterans Association.

A small contribution made in respect and honor of veterans.

When you buy a coloring book, you help carry the message of remembrance and peace forward.

Contribution arrangement and recipient details will be documented transparently as the project develops.
