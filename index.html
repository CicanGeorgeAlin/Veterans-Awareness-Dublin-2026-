A personal memory at the heart of Veteran Awareness Dublin 2026. His memory became the reason to create something for peace, remembrance and respect.

The message

Instead of going to war and taking weapons, we take crayons and we color.

A simple idea: remember those who served while creating something that points toward peace rather than conflict.

The project

Memory becomes something we can carry forward.

Veteran Awareness Dublin 2026 connects remembrance with creativity. Through coloring, photographs and personal symbols, the project keeps memories visible while giving people a peaceful way to participate.

Dublin · Remembrance Remembering veterans and the people whose lives are connected to the history of service and sacrifice.
Roots

Where memory begins.

Every memorial starts somewhere: with a person, a photograph, a place, or a story that should not be forgotten.

CICAN knot

A symbol made by hand.

The CICAN knot is a handmade commemorative symbol created from string and incorporating a cross knot. It represents memory, connection and something that can be physically carried forward.
