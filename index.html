The idea

Instead of going to war and taking weapons, we take crayons and we color.

A simple idea: remember those who served while creating something that points toward peace rather than conflict.

Personal story

Memory becomes something we can carry forward.

Veteran Awareness Dublin 2026 connects remembrance with creativity. It is a personal project built around the belief that stories of service should be remembered, shared and carried forward in peaceful ways.

Something that can be carried forward.
